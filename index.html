Water Damage In Little Elm

What Little Elm Properties Face

Little Elm sits on the northern and eastern shores of Lewisville Lake in Denton County, and that shapes the water problems we see. The town grew from 3,646 residents in 2000 to 46,453 in 2020, so a large share of its housing was built during that boom. Builder-era supply lines, washer hoses and water heaters are now reaching the end of their service lives, and checking them once a year is cheap insurance against a flood in the hallway.

The lake is also a reminder that water can arrive from outside. When Lewisville Lake climbed to 537 feet in June 2015, the beach at Little Elm Park went underwater. Homes close to the shoreline are worth checking against the FEMA flood map, and a talk with your insurer about flood coverage is cheap compared with learning about the gap after a loss.

Weather adds its own pattern. The National Weather Service reports a normal 37.01 inches of precipitation a year at Dallas/Fort Worth, with May the wettest month at 4.78 inches, and about 29 days a year at or below freezing. The February 2021 freeze dropped DFW to -2 degrees Fahrenheit on February 16. Know where your main water shutoff is before the next hard freeze, and insulate any pipe that runs along an exterior wall.

Calls come from every part of town, from the older Lakefront district near Eldorado Parkway and Main Street to homes along the University Drive corridor and the FM 423 and FM 720 neighborhoods. Tell the dispatcher which area you are in. It helps the crew plan the route and the equipment. Our base is at 2700 East Eldorado Parkway, and ZIP code 75068 is home territory.

Types Of Water

Not All Water Damage Is The Same

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard sorts water into three categories, and the category decides how the job is handled. A burst supply line floods with clean water, but the same water becomes gray after it has soaked carpet and sat for a day or two. Sewage and flood water are in a different class entirely and need protective equipment from the first minute.

You do not have to name the category when you call. Describe where the water came from and what it touched, and the dispatcher will sort it out with you. The service pages for water damage cleanup and flood damage restoration go deeper on each one.

CategoryTypical sourceWhat usually happens
Category 1Clean source water, such as a broken supply line or an overflowing sinkExtract, dry and monitor. Most materials can be saved if the response is quick.
Category 2Gray water, such as discharge from a washer or dishwasherExtract, clean and sanitize. Porous materials like carpet pad are often removed.
Category 3Black water, such as sewage or rising flood waterContainment, protective gear and removal of porous materials. Stay out until a crew arrives.

Insurance Claims

Documentation That Helps Your Claim

Photos, Moisture Logs And Scope Notes

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage is usually excluded. Check your policy or ask your insurer. We document the loss with photos, moisture readings and a written scope so your adjuster has something concrete to review.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can a crew get to my Little Elm home?

The dispatch line answers 24 hours a day, and we offer same-day response when available. We do not promise an arrival time, because distance, weather and call volume change it. The dispatcher tells you what to expect when you call.

Do you handle water emergencies at night or on weekends in Little Elm?

Yes, and nights are when many burst pipes and overflowing appliances are discovered. The line is staffed at 2 a.m. on a Sunday the same as at noon on a Tuesday, so call the moment you see water.

Will you explain the price before work starts?

Yes. The scope of work and how the price is set are explained before any work begins. A real number depends on the water category, the area affected, the materials involved and how many days of drying the structure needs.

Does homeowners insurance pay for water damage?

Many policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, such as a burst pipe or a failed washer hose. Gradual leaks usually are not covered, and flood damage generally needs a separate flood policy. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and photograph everything before cleanup.

Do you serve areas outside Little Elm?

Yes. Dispatch from Little Elm covers Frisco, The Colony, Prosper and Aubrey, plus other nearby Denton County addresses. Call and give the dispatcher your address.

Is restoration the same as waterproofing?

No. Restoration removes water and repairs damage after a loss. Waterproofing and foundation work prevent future intrusion and are a different trade. If a recurring leak points to a drainage or foundation problem, we will say so.
