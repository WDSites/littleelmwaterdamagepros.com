When a pressurized line has already let go

A burst pipe is an emergency because the water does not stop on its own, and our 24/7 dispatch line is built for it. Before anyone arrives, close the main shutoff, then open a faucet at the lowest point in the house to bleed off the pressure left in the pipes. Lift rugs, boxes and electronics off the floor, and keep kids and pets out of the wet rooms.

Cut power to the affected rooms only if the breaker panel is dry and you can reach it. Take photos of the wet areas before anything moves. Call us when the water is stopped. A crew can often be sent the same day, including after hours.

Supply water starts out clean, which is why crews call it Category 1. The trouble is time. Water that sits in carpet, pad and drywall picks up dirt and bacteria, and under ANSI/IICRC S500 logic it slides from Category 1 toward Category 2 as the hours pass. In plain words, a clean leak caught fast is cheaper to dry than the same leak found next week.

Ceiling stain spreading or a wall that feels cold and damp

Most homeowners notice a burst line by its side effects. A brown ring grows on the ceiling, paint bubbles on a wall, the floor feels spongy, or you hear water running when every fixture is off. Those are symptoms of pressurized water hidden inside a cavity, and the trade term for the fix is water damage mitigation: find the water, remove it and dry the structure before it rots.

Electrical, gas and shutoff safety

Before a crew reaches you, these are the steps that matter most. Water and electricity do not mix, and a ceiling that has been soaking can hold a lot of weight. If water is dripping from a light fixture, running down an outlet or pooling near the panel, stay out of that room. Call the power company or 911 first, then call us.

  • Smell gas or hear hissing near a water heater or range? Leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before anything else.
  • A bulging, sagging ceiling can drop without warning. Do not stand under it, and do not poke it with a broom unless a crew tells you how.
  • If you cannot find the main shutoff, do not guess. Ask the Town of Little Elm about your account and meter location, and call a licensed plumber to stop the flow.
  • Water that has touched a wall socket stays off limits until a licensed electrician clears it.

How we dry out after a burst line

We start with extraction units on standing water and soaked carpet. Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras then show which walls and floors hold water behind the surface, because a wall that looks dry can still be wet inside.

Wet insulation and saturated drywall come out, and the cavity opens so air can reach the studs. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run for the days it takes to bring materials back to dry, and we recheck readings each visit. Insurance fit and mistakes to avoid

Texas Department of Insurance guidance describes sudden, accidental water damage as the kind most home policies cover, and it tells owners not to make permanent repairs before the insurer sees the damage. Temporary steps are fine. Stop the water, dry the area, and keep receipts for what you spend.

  • Waiting to see whether the stain gets bigger. Wet drywall and insulation can go bad within days.
  • Closing a wall back up before the cavity is dry, which traps moisture against the framing.
  • Throwing out the broken fitting. Your insurer or plumber may want to see it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is emergency burst pipe water damage cleanup available in Little Elm overnight?

Yes. Calls are answered around the clock, so a pipe that splits at 2 a.m. or on a holiday weekend gets a call answered. Same-day visits are possible depending on the schedule. Shut off the main and bleed pressure from a low faucet while you wait.

Should I shut off the main water valve even if only one fixture leaks?

Yes, if the leak is under pressure and you cannot reach a fixture shutoff fast. The main stops the flow to the whole house. Open a low faucet afterward to relieve the pressure left in the lines.

How do I know if the wall behind the leak is still wet?

Paint that bubbles, a cool spot, a musty smell or baseboards that swell are signs. Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras read inside the wall without cutting first. A dry surface is not proof of a dry cavity.

Do you repair the broken pipe?

No. A licensed plumber repairs or replaces the line. We extract the water, open the wet cavities, dry the structure and help you scope the rebuild.
