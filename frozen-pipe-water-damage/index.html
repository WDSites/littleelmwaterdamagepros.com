When a thaw turns into active flooding

If water is pouring after a freeze, treat it as an emergency, and know that our 24/7 dispatch line takes these calls. Close the main shutoff first, since a split pipe that thawed is now a live leak. Move valuables and electronics off the floor, keep pets and children out of wet rooms, and photograph the damage.

Kill power to the wet area only when the panel sits in a dry spot and reachable. We try to book a visit the same day, including nights and weekends.

The cruel part of a freeze loss is the delay. Ice plugs the crack, so the house looks fine and the meter sits still. Hours later the temperature climbs, the plug melts and water sprays into a wall or attic that nobody was watching.

Frozen lines in North Texas are rare but real

NWS Dallas/Fort Worth climate normals show an average of 29.2 days a year with a low at or below 32 F. Most of those nights are mild, and many Little Elm homes never see a pipe problem. The danger sits in the hard freezes, as in February 2021, when the DFW low reached minus 2 F.

Lines that run through attics, exterior walls and garages are the ones exposed to a hard freeze. Know where yours run before the forecast turns, because a short deep freeze is enough to split them.

Stain on the ceiling after a cold night

Homeowners often find a freeze loss by a damp patch on the ceiling, a bubbling wall below an attic run, low water pressure, or a hose bib that drips inside the garage wall. Those are signs of a split line releasing water into a cavity, and the trade term for the response is water mitigation: remove the water and dry the structure.

Thawing safety and electrical caution

Never use an open flame, torch or heat gun on a frozen pipe. Fire, scorched framing and a split line that still leaks is a bad trade. Shut the main before you thaw anything, then warm the area slowly with a hair dryer or warm towels, working from the faucet end back toward the frozen section.

  • Attic wiring and insulation can be wet and hidden. Do not climb into a wet attic, and do not touch light fixtures or junction boxes.
  • Water near an outlet, panel or ceiling fan means stay out. Call the power company or 911 before calling us.
  • A gas smell means leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first.
  • Ceilings that sag with trapped water can drop. Keep clear and stay out of the room.

How we dry after the ice melts

We begin with extraction units on standing water, then use moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras to trace the path of water through ceilings and walls. Freeze splits often release water in two or three spots, so the survey matters.

Soaked insulation and drywall are removed, cavities opened, and air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run until readings return to a dry standard. The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard guides that drying work, and you should ask any company whether its crews follow it. Our water mitigation page explains each drying phase.

Freeze prevention and mistakes to avoid

  • Let faucets on exterior-wall lines drip when a hard freeze is forecast.
  • Insulate exposed lines in attics and garages and cover outdoor hose bibs.
  • Find your main shutoff before winter, and make sure everyone in the house knows where it is.
  • Leave the heat on, at a reasonable setting, when you travel.
  • Do not chip ice off a pipe or crank the heat to maximum, which can crack a weakened section.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is emergency frozen pipe water damage restoration available in Little Elm on a weekend morning?

Yes. The line stays open all night, and thaw floods often start overnight or on weekends. Ask us about a same-day visit. Shut the main valve and stay out of rooms with water near electrical.

My pipes froze but nothing is leaking yet. What should I do?

Shut the main before you thaw anything, then thaw slowly with warm air. Never use a torch or open flame. Check for splits as the ice melts, since a crack may only show once water can move.

Why did my house flood hours after the temperature rose?

Ice often plugs a split pipe, so no water escapes until it melts. When the plug goes, the pipe leaks under full pressure into a wall or attic.

Does a hose bib freeze damage reach inside?

It can. A split bib on the garage or an exterior wall can leak into the wall cavity behind it, and the damp patch shows up on the interior side.

Does insurance cover frozen pipe damage?

Many policies cover sudden, accidental pipe damage. Check your policy and ask your insurer about freeze conditions, because wording varies. Report hidden damage within days of first seeing it.

Can sprinkler or irrigation backflow lines flood my house?

Yes, if the line ties into the house or garage and splits when frozen. We dry the affected rooms, and an irrigation or plumbing contractor repairs the system.
