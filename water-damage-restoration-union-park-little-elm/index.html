Where Union Park sits, and why your address matters

Union Park is a Hillwood master-planned community on the Little Elm side of US 380. The Town of Little Elm lists the Union Park homeowners association in its HOA directory, and Dallas News reported in 2014 that Hillwood had broken ground on the subdivision. Homes have gone up in phases since then, so the neighborhood is a run of recent builds rather than one construction year.

Local listing sources say most Union Park homes are in Little Elm and a few are in the city of Aubrey, and listings often carry the 76227 ZIP code. A ZIP code does not tell you which city handles your street or your water line. Check your tax bill or ask your HOA before you call the wrong office.

What the first sign usually looks like

A brown ring spreading on the ceiling under an upstairs bathroom, a baseboard that feels cool and damp behind the dishwasher, a puddle under the water heater and a musty smell in a closet are the signs people describe. Each one means water is moving somewhere you cannot see. Restoration starts by finding that path with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera, then drying it on purpose.

Safety steps before anyone starts drying

Water supply

Close the main valve only if you can reach it without standing in water. If you cannot, leave it alone and call a plumber or the Town.

Electricity

Do not touch switches, cords or the panel while water is near them. A breaker should go off only if the panel is dry and reachable without stepping in water.

Gas and sparks

A gas smell or a spark means everyone goes outside. Call 911 or the gas utility from outside the house.

Sewage and storm water

Keep people and pets out. Household fans and ordinary vacuums do not make contaminated water safe.

Breathing and mold smells

Move children, pets and anyone with breathing trouble away from wet rooms and musty air until a professional has looked.

How we dry a Union Park home

If water is standing, extraction units pull it out first. We then map the wet area with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera, and remove carpet pad, soaked insulation or swollen drywall that cannot be dried in place. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run while readings are logged, guided by ANSI/IICRC S500, the trade's published reference for water damage work.

Water from a clean supply line is handled differently from dishwasher discharge or sewage, and that category decides how much material comes out. Our water mitigation page covers the early steps and structural drying explains the equipment and the readings.

The 55+ section and single-story layouts

Dallas News reported in 2017 that Del Webb planned about 600 homes for residents 55 and older inside Union Park. In a single-story plan a leak spreads across one floor instead of dripping into a room below, so finding the direction the water travels matters most.

Make sure more than one person in the household knows where the main shutoff and the water heater valve are. If a leak starts while you are away, a neighbor or relative can act sooner. Our appliance leak water damage page explains what gets wet behind a washer, dishwasher or refrigerator.

Ponds, parks and the flood question

Hillwood describes a central park of about 30 acres with ponds, trails and woods. Water features are part of the setting, but a pond nearby does not mean your lot floods. We have not verified FEMA flood-zone status for Union Park as a neighborhood, so check the FEMA map for your own address.

According to Texas Department of Insurance guidance, a home policy does not pay for floods, so a flood is a matter for its own flood policy. Your agent can explain which situations your policy treats as sudden and accidental and which it treats as flood.

Records that help a claim

Texas Department of Insurance guidance tells homeowners to stop the water and start drying, then record what happened with photos or video, a list of damaged property and every receipt. Damaged items should stay until the adjuster has seen them, and damage you discover later usually has to be reported within days of spotting it. Moisture readings and an itemized scope from our crew can support your record, but coverage depends on the cause and your policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Union Park in Little Elm or Aubrey?

Most of it is in Little Elm and the Town lists the Union Park HOA, but local listing sources say a few homes are in Aubrey. Your tax bill or HOA can confirm which city covers your street.

Does a builder warranty replace a restoration visit?

Not necessarily. Warranty terms differ, so ask the builder in writing what it covers and whether it wants to inspect first. Do not wait on that answer to stop the water and photograph the damage.

Who handles water problems in the park or other common areas?

Common areas are normally an HOA matter, so report them to your association or its management company. We work on the inside of your home.

Can you dry a home in the 55+ section?

Yes. The process is the same, and you can call and describe the layout and what got wet. Tell the dispatcher if anyone in the home has trouble moving around so the crew can plan access.
