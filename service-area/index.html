Dispatched From Little Elm

Our base is at 2700 East Eldorado Parkway in Little Elm, Texas, ZIP code 75068. Crews and equipment are sent from this part of Denton County to properties in town and to the neighboring cities listed below. The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day, and we offer same-day response when available.

Inside Little Elm, the calls we get span the Lakefront district near Cottonwood Park, the University Drive corridor in the north, and subdivisions served by Eldorado Parkway, FM 423 and FM 720. Tell the dispatcher your cross streets and what is wet, and the crew plans from there.

Little Elm Neighborhoods

Inside Little Elm, four communities have their own pages: Union Park, Sunset Pointe, Valencia on the Lake and Spiritas Ranch. Other Little Elm neighborhoods are served the same way, and a call with your cross streets is all the dispatcher needs.

How Coverage Works

The towns above are the places we write about in detail because their housing, water systems and weather exposure differ in ways that change the work. A town page is not a boundary line. If you live a few streets past a town limit, call anyway.

We also take calls from Denton, Plano, Lewisville and Celina, and from the community of Hackberry. These places do not have pages of their own, so a short call to confirm your address is the quickest route.

ZIP codes help the dispatcher route the call. They are not a promise of arrival time, and no page on this site guarantees one.

Getting Around The Area

FM 423 ties Little Elm to Frisco and The Colony, and US 380 carries traffic east and west along the northern edge of the area toward Prosper and Aubrey. Crews know these corridors, and the dispatcher can usually tell you which route and which equipment a call needs once you give an address.

Lewisville Lake sits between several of these towns, which is why a drive that looks short on a map can take a different path. That is another reason we avoid printing drive times on this site. The dispatcher gives you the plan that fits your address and the day.

If you are calling for a rental property, a vacation house or a relative who lives in one of these towns, say so. The dispatcher will ask who can grant access and who approves the work.

Questions We Hear From The Neighboring Towns

People in Frisco often ask whether a crew based in Little Elm is too far away to help. People in Prosper and Aubrey ask the same thing in different words. The honest answer is that distance affects timing, not whether we dispatch, and the dispatcher will tell you what to expect for your address.

People in The Colony, where the lake shapes so much of the housing, tend to ask about flood coverage and the difference between sudden leaks and slow ones. Those answers live on the town page and on the flood damage restoration page.

Before You Call From Out Of Town

Have the street address, the nearest cross streets and a short description of the water ready. Photos taken on your phone help too, and you can describe them on the call. Your insurer will want the same photos later, so taking them early saves a step.

Which Service Do You Need

Wherever you are, the first question is whether water is still present. If it is, emergency water removal and water mitigation come first. After flooding from a storm or a backed-up sewer line, read about flood damage restoration. For a full list, see the services page, or contact us directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if you cover my address?

Call the 24/7 line and give the dispatcher your street address. Coverage is confirmed on the call, and most Denton County addresses near the towns below are served.

Do you serve Denton, Lewisville, Plano or Celina?

These nearby cities are served on request. They do not have their own pages yet, so call and give the dispatcher your address.

Are ZIP codes a coverage guarantee?

No. The ZIP codes on these pages describe the areas we dispatch to, and the dispatcher confirms each address when you call.

Is the service area the same for mold work?

Mostly yes. Mold remediation follows the same dispatch area, and the dispatcher will explain how assessment and remediation are handled for your address.

Need Help Right Now?

The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day. Call and a dispatcher will take it from there.
