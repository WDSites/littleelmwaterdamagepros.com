When rain is coming through the ceiling right now

A ceiling that drips or bulges during a storm is an emergency, and our 24/7 dispatch line is built for it. Roof leak safety: bulging ceilings, wet wiring and ladders

Stay clear of a sagging ceiling

Wet drywall is heavy, and a bulging ceiling holds a lot of trapped water. It can drop without warning. Do not stand under it, do not poke it to drain it, and keep children and pets out of the room. Call us and keep clear until a crew has assessed it.

Water near outlets, ceiling fans or a breaker panel means nobody goes in. If you smell gas or see sparks, leave and call 911 or the utility first, then call us. Do not climb onto a wet roof to look for the hole.

How roof water travels through the house

Water rarely drips straight down from the hole. It runs along the roof deck, soaks the attic insulation, follows a rafter or pipe, and lands on the ceiling drywall far from the entry point. From there it can slide into a wall cavity and reach the floor.

How we dry and restore after the roofer

Crews start with moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras, which find wet drywall and insulation that look dry from below. Saturated attic insulation comes out first because it keeps feeding water into the ceiling. Soaked ceiling board is cut away in a controlled way, so the rest of the room stays protected.

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers then dry the framing, deck underside and wall cavities. We follow ANSI/IICRC S500, the published water damage restoration standard, and you can ask any company whether its crews do the same. Daily readings tell us when the structure is dry.

The split of trades is simple. A roofer repairs the roof, and we dry the inside. Once the structure is dry, finishing work such as new drywall, texture and paint belongs to water damage repair.

Tarps and temporary repairs before the adjuster visits

A tarp is a temporary measure, and Texas Department of Insurance guidance lists tarping as a reasonable temporary repair. The same guidance says to hold off on permanent repairs until your insurance company has seen the damage. That means the roofer can tarp today, but a full replacement waits for the adjuster.

Keep receipts for the tarp, photograph the wet areas, and save damaged items until the adjuster looks at them. If the storm that opened the roof also hit windows or siding, our storm water damage page covers wind-driven rain.

Why roof leaks show up in May and October

NWS Dallas/Fort Worth climate normals show just over 37 inches of precipitation in an average year, with May's 4.78 inches and October's 4.37 inches as the wettest months. Those are the two months when a worn flashing or a lifted shingle gets tested the hardest. Have the roof looked at in April and September, and check the attic with a flashlight after the first big rain.

Mistakes that make a roof leak worse

  • Leaving wet attic insulation in place because the ceiling looks fine.
  • Poking a bulging ceiling or standing under it.
  • Painting over a stain before the framing is dry.
  • Making permanent roof repairs before the adjuster sees the damage.
  • Waiting weeks to report hidden damage. Texas Department of Insurance says hidden water damage usually must be reported within days after you first see it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is emergency roof leak water cleanup available in Little Elm during a holiday weekend?

Yes. Our team takes calls at any hour, and same-day visits are possible depending on the schedule, including overnight storms. Tell us if a ceiling is bulging so the crew knows what to expect.

Why is the ceiling stain nowhere near the leak?

Water follows the roof deck, rafters and pipes before it drops. The stain shows where it finally reached drywall, not where the roof opened.

Should I poke a hole in a bulging ceiling?

No. Stay out from under it and call us. A ceiling holding water can fall without warning, and a crew can assess it safely.

Do you repair the roof?

No. A roofer fixes the roof. We dry the attic, ceiling and walls, and we remove what water ruined.

Does wet attic insulation always have to come out?

Soaked batts usually do, because they hold water against the ceiling. Lightly damp sections may dry in place, and meter readings decide.

Will the stain go away on its own once the roof is fixed?

The stain stays and the wet materials behind it can keep growing damage. The structure needs drying, and then the ceiling needs repair.
