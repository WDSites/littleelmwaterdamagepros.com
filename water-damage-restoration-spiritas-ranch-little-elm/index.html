A slow leak is the one you miss

A faint ceiling shadow, a baseboard that has lifted at one corner, a damp smell from a closet or a bill that creeps up with no explanation all point to a slow leak. These are moisture problems with a hidden source. A leak you cannot find still needs a plumber for the repair, and we then dry what it soaked.

If your home sits on a slab, a leak under it can wet flooring from below, and our slab leak water damage page explains how that is handled. A dripping air handler or a clogged condensate line has its own steps on AC leak water damage repair.

Safety steps while you wait

Shut off what you can

Close the valve at the fixture, the appliance or the main. Do not step into standing water to reach one.

Electricity

Keep clear of outlets, cords and the panel while water is near, and never reach for a breaker with wet hands or wet feet.

Gas and heat

Leave for any gas smell and call 911 or the gas utility from outside. A leaking water heater needs its power or gas control off.

Contaminated water

Water from a drain, toilet or outside source is not safe to handle. Keep everyone out.

How restoration works from extraction to dry readings

Extraction units remove standing water first. We then check walls, subfloor and ceilings with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera and take out materials that cannot be saved. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the structure, and readings are logged daily using ANSI/IICRC S500, the written standard the trade follows for water losses.

For wet drywall and trim, see wall and drywall water damage repair. If water is standing now, our emergency water removal page explains the pumping stage.

Freeze nights and outdoor lines

National Weather Service climate normals for Dallas and Fort Worth show about 29 days a year with a low at or below freezing. Disconnect hoses and cover outdoor spigots before a hard freeze, and learn where your main shutoff is before you need it.

Insurance paperwork

Under Texas Department of Insurance guidance a policy normally responds to sudden and accidental water but often excludes slow leaks and the mold that follows. Keep a photo record, a list of damaged property and the receipts, and keep damaged items for the adjuster. Neighborhood-level FEMA flood-zone status for Spiritas Ranch is not verified here, so use the FEMA map for your address if flooding is a worry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Spiritas Ranch part of Little Elm?

The Town of Little Elm lists the combined Spiritas Ranch and Spiritas East HOA in its HOA directory. Confirm your own address with your tax bill, because school districts and ZIP codes do not mark city limits.

Does my builder warranty cover water damage?

Warranties differ by builder and by item. Send the warranty office a written question about coverage and any inspection it wants, and keep stopping the water and taking photos in the meantime.

Which agency handles a leak in the street?

The Town of Little Elm bills water to residents, so your bill shows who to call about a line in the street. A leak inside the house is the homeowner's to repair with a plumber.

What if I cannot find where the water is coming from?

Call and describe the stain or the smell. Meters and a thermal camera can show where the wet area is, and a plumber then repairs the source.
