When a water heater is leaking right now

A heater that is actively leaking is an emergency, and our 24/7 dispatch line takes these calls. Close the cold-water inlet valve on top of the tank, which stops new water from refilling it. Keep everyone away from hot water, lift items off the floor, and photograph the leak and the tank label.

A same-day visit is often possible, after hours included. A plumber has to replace the heater, so call one too. We handle the water that got out.

Heater safety: power, gas and scalding water

Before a crew arrives, take these steps in this order. Electric heater: turn the breaker off only if the panel area is dry and you can reach it without standing in water. Gas heater: turn the control to off or pilot, and if you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first. Never touch wet electrical components.

  • Leaking water from the tank or relief pipe can be scalding. Keep skin clear of it.
  • Do not open the drain valve on a hot tank without knowing how.
  • Water that has reached outlets, switches or the panel stays off limits until an electrician clears it.
  • A bulging ceiling under an attic heater can drop without warning. Stay out.

Where the heater sits changes what gets wet

The location decides the damage. A heater in a garage may flood the slab and soak boxes, drywall edges and stored items. A closet or utility room can push water into the flooring and the walls beside it. If the heater sits in an attic, water can come through the ceiling and run down walls into the rooms below.

Relief valve discharge, fitting leak or failing tank

A tripped relief valve discharges to a pipe and is usually a pressure or temperature event. A failed drain pan or line sends water out without anyone noticing. Corroded tanks seep slowly. Flex line failures at the heater spray water from the connectors on top.

Water from the supply side is usually Category 1 at first, meaning clean. A heater that has leaked slowly for weeks can leave staining, soft flooring and mold, and the water in that case is no longer clean. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard that guides how those categories are handled.

How we dry after a heater leak

We start by extracting water with extraction units, then use moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras to find where water traveled under flooring and into walls. Wet insulation and drywall come out, and subfloor edges are opened for airflow.

Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run for the days needed to reach dry readings. If staining or growth is present from a slow leak, mold remediation may be a separate step. Our water damage cleanup page covers the broader cleanup sequence.

Restoration is not plumbing. A licensed plumber replaces the heater and fixes the valves, and we dry and restore the house.

Heater age, your policy and Little Elm homes

Census counts put Little Elm at 25,898 residents in 2010 and 46,453 in 2020. Many homes were built or renovated in that window, so ask when your heater was installed and look at the date on its label. An older tank is closer to the end of its life and worth watching for rust at the base.

Texas Department of Insurance guidance separates sudden and accidental water damage from gradual leaks and seepage. A slow seep from tank corrosion often falls on the gradual side, while a failed fitting or burst connector tends to look sudden. Your adjuster decides, so document everything and ask your insurer how your policy reads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is emergency water heater leak cleanup available in Little Elm after midnight?

Yes. Weekend and overnight calls are normal for this work, and we work to schedule the same day where we can. Close the cold-water inlet valve and stay away from hot water while you wait.

What is a relief valve discharge versus a real leak?

The relief valve opens to release excess pressure or temperature and sends water down a discharge pipe. That can happen once and stop. A leak is water escaping from the tank body, a fitting or a connector, and it keeps going. Ask a plumber to check the valve either way.

Should I drain the tank myself?

Usually no, unless a plumber tells you to. The water can be scalding, and draining a hot tank wrong can cause burns. Shut the cold inlet, turn off power or gas controls, and call a licensed plumber.

Can a slow heater leak cause mold?

It can. Moisture that sits in drywall, wood and flooring for weeks supports growth. Testing and remediation follow ANSI/IICRC S520, and health questions belong with a physician.

Will you replace the heater?

No. A licensed plumber replaces the heater. We extract the water, dry the structure and help you scope any repair.

Does my policy cover a leaking water heater?

Sudden failures are often covered, and slow seepage often is not. Check your policy and ask your insurer, and keep the old parts for the adjuster.
