When a leak under the floor is active right now

A pressurized line under your slab never stops leaking, so treat it as an emergency. Our 24/7 dispatch line covers nights and weekends. While you wait, shut off the main water valve, lift rugs and furniture off wet flooring, and keep people away from any area where the floor feels hot or the carpet squishes.

If the panel is dry and easy to reach, cut power to wet rooms only. Photograph the damage and note the time you first noticed it. A same-day visit is often possible.

Warm floor, hissing walls, a bill that jumped

Slab leaks announce themselves in small ways. A warm patch on the tile, the sound of running water with every fixture off, a spike on the water bill, wet carpet along a slab edge, or flooring that lifts and cracks. That pattern means water under or inside the concrete is reaching the surface. The trade term is slab leak, and the restoration side is structural drying.

Slab-on-grade homes and how this leak happens

Many homes in this part of North Texas are built slab-on-grade, with supply lines running through or beneath the concrete. That is a common and honest arrangement, so a slab leak is a real case here. Lines can corrode, rub against gravel, or fail at a joint.

Slab leak safety: hot water, electricity and the main valve

Shut it off before anything else

Close the main water valve to stop the supply. A leak on the hot side can make the floor warm to the touch, so watch for scalding water at the surface. Water near outlets, floor boxes or a breaker panel means nobody enters.

If you smell gas, leave and call 911 or the gas company first, then call us. Do not drill, jackhammer or pry at the slab yourself.

Who does what: the plumber and the drying crew

Before you do anything else, run through the list below. A plumber locates the leak with leak detection and repairs it by fixing the line or rerouting it through the attic or walls. We do not break the slab to repair a pipe. Our job is to dry the slab-top flooring, subfloor and wall bottoms the water reached.

Cracks, heaving or soil movement belong to a foundation specialist. Restoration is not foundation repair, and a wet slab can be a symptom of movement or a cause of it. We will tell you plainly when a question sits outside our trade.

How we map and dry wet flooring

Crews use moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras to map wet flooring and wall bottoms, since water spreads sideways across a slab. Wet carpet and pad are lifted. Baseboards come off, and low drywall is cut when it has wicked water.

Hardwood and tile need special care. Boards are pulled or drilled in controlled ways so air reaches the subfloor beneath them. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the room, and readings are logged daily following ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry's published guide for restoring water-damaged structures.

Hidden water, reporting windows and the water bill

Texas Department of Insurance guidance says hidden water damage should be reported within days after you first see it. Write down the date you noticed a warm floor, a sound or a bill jump. The Town of Little Elm bills water, so a sudden spike is a clue, and you can ask the Town about your account.

Insurance often turns on whether a leak was a sudden burst or a slow seep. Sudden, accidental water is often covered, while gradual damage often is not, so bring your policy to your insurer's agent. If the line itself failed fast, our burst pipe water damage page covers that pattern.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is emergency slab leak cleanup available in Little Elm in the middle of the night?

Yes. Dispatch never closes, and we work to schedule the same day where we can. Shut off the main water valve while you wait.

How do I know I have a slab leak?

Common signs are a warm floor spot, running water sounds with everything off, a jump in the water bill, wet carpet at the slab edge, or lifting flooring. A plumber confirms it with leak detection.

Do you fix the pipe under the slab?

No. A plumber locates and repairs or reroutes the line. We dry the flooring, subfloor and wall bottoms.

Can you dry hardwood or tile over a slab leak?

Often yes, with controlled lifting or drilling so air reaches the subfloor. Meter readings decide what can stay.

Is a slab leak the same as foundation damage?

No. A slab leak is a plumbing failure. Cracks or movement should go to a foundation specialist.

Will my insurance cover a slab leak?

It depends on your policy and on whether the leak was sudden or gradual. Ask your insurer, and report hidden damage within days of seeing it.
